About the Project

AquaHub Climate

A climate and agroclimatic atlas supporting agriculture across the Douro river basin, developed under the Interreg VI-A POCTEP programme.

Background

Interreg VI-A POCTEP

The project is part of Action A.1.1 of the Interreg VI-A POCTEP programme (Spain-Portugal Cross-Border Cooperation Programme), developed at the Universidade de Trás-os-Montes e Alto Douro (UTAD), supported by a research fellowship (BI/UTAD/46/2026). The goal is to build a climate and agroclimatic atlas for the whole Douro river basin, covering Portugal and Spain from the outset — not a phased rollout starting in one country and expanding to the other.

Objectives

What the project delivers

Two complementary outputs: an atlas — the complete, systematic set of climate data and maps for the territory — and an interactive platform to explore that data by zone, period and climate scenario. The platform is already available; the full atlas, including the crop-specific bioclimatic indices, is being built together with the research team.

Development of the data pipeline started with the Douro zone, used to validate the methodology against real climate and administrative data before applying the same process to the other four zones as their data becomes available — a technical starting point, not a statement about which zone matters most.

Target crops

Four priority crops

The agroclimatic analysis focuses on the crops with the greatest economic and cultural relevance for the region.

Grapevine

The Douro region's flagship crop — the most extensively studied in terms of viticultural bioclimatic indices.

Olive

Sensitive to chilling requirements and frost risk.

Almond

Early flowering, making it particularly exposed to late frost events.

Cherry

Also sensitive to chilling requirements and water availability.

Methodology

Data and scientific transparency

The climate baseline uses CHELSA v2.1 climatologies (1981-2010 historical, plus SSP1-2.6/SSP5-8.5 future scenarios), matched against official administrative boundaries — Portugal's Carta Administrativa Oficial (CAOP) and Eurostat's NUTS boundaries (GISCO) for Spain. Several scientific decisions are still pending confirmation from the research team: the definitive dataset, which Global Climate Models (GCMs) to use, and the crop-specific bioclimatic indices — which the supervising professor is calculating directly. The project explicitly documents what has been validated and what remains pending, rather than presenting unconfirmed work as final.
