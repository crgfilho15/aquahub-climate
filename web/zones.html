Intervention Zones

Five zones, two countries

The project covers three zones in Portugal and two in Spain, reflecting the Douro river basin on both sides of the border — all part of the same scope from the start.

Portugal

Three NUTS III zones

Defined at municipality level, with climate data processed from Portugal's Carta Administrativa Oficial (CAOP) and the CHELSA climatologies.

Douro

The first zone processed, used to validate the data pipeline — 19 municipalities, with a validated historical baseline and the project's first set of future (SSP) scenarios.

real data available

Terras de Trás-os-Montes

The second Portuguese zone built, following the same historical validation process as Douro.

real data available

Beira Interior

A combination of the "Beira Baixa" and "Beiras e Serra da Estrela" NUTS III units — the closest match to the project's historical intervention area.

real data available

Spain

Two autonomous communities

Defined at NUTS II level (whole region, no municipality subdivision), with boundaries sourced from Eurostat/GISCO.

Castilla y León

Administrative boundary already confirmed and integrated into the map; climate data processing for the region is still in development.

climate data pending

Extremadura

Same status as Castilla y León — boundary ready, climate data still to be processed.

climate data pending
